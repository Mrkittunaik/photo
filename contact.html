Frequently Asked

How far in advance should we book? +

Most couples book 6–9 months ahead, especially for wedding-season dates (Nov–Feb) and destination weddings. Popular dates fill quickly, so we recommend enquiring as soon as your date is fixed.

Do you travel for destination weddings? +

Yes — we shoot across India and internationally. Travel and stay are quoted separately based on location and are added to your package cost.

How long until we receive our photos? +

A curated sneak-peek gallery is delivered within 72 hours. The fully edited gallery and film follow within 4–6 weeks, depending on package.

Can we customize a package? +

Absolutely. Every wedding is different — let us know your rituals, guest count and must-have deliverables and we'll build a quote around it.
